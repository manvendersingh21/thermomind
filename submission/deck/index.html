Safety architecture

Read-only by construction

No control write path

No PLC, SCADA, DCS or historian writes. No setpoints, trips or start commands, ever.

Validator after the model

Rejects fabricated evidence IDs, invented dates and control language. One rewrite, then needs_review.

Human review

Every report is draft_requires_human_review. Severity ranks review priority, not alarms.

Replayable

Every model request, tool call, result and validation is stored.

Limits stated

Single-user prototype, no auth; not hardened for an OT network. The validator guarantees provenance and form, not correct reasoning.

Market and buyer · hypotheses, not claims

The case file, not the alarm, is the unit of value

Buyer (hypothesis)

IPPs, O&M contractors and utility reliability groups; per-plant or per-turbine annual pilot. Pricing comes from pilots.

Champion

The performance / reliability engineer who triages deviations in spreadsheets today.

Gatekeepers

Plant IT / OT security and OEM relationship owners. Exported data only, no OT integration.

Beside incumbents, not against them

Siemens Energy, GE Vernova and AspenTech offer mature monitoring embedded in service agreements. Our hypothesis: a vendor-neutral, auditable case built on exported data complements it. We have no customers and no evidence yet that engineers prefer this.

Risks named in docs/MARKET.md: one plant's data, confounding from dispatch, OT security, OEM data restrictions, long sales cycles.

Path to pilot

Four steps, then measure

1. Pick one site

One gas turbine, one engineer, a read-only offline historian export.

2. Shadow mode, 8-12 weeks

Run on the site's history; retrain baseline chronologically; engineers compare with their spreadsheets.

3. Measure

Against the metrics at right.

4. Decide on price

If metrics hold, per-turbine pilot after the OT security assessment.
